The app does not require an internet connection and does not send data to us or to any third party. It contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no user accounts. Any settings the app keeps, such as volume levels, stay on your device.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking tools, and loads no external fonts or scripts.

If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them once the matter is closed, unless legal retention duties apply.

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under the GDPR. To exercise them, email mono-app@gmx.de.
